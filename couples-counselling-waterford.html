Couples & Marriage Counselling in Waterford

For the conversations that keep going in circles, and the ones that never quite start

Couples counselling gives you and your partner a space to have the conversations that keep breaking down at home, with someone in the room whose job is to make sure both of you are heard. I offer couples and marriage counselling in Waterford, and online, at €100 for a 60-minute session.

What couples bring

Some couples arrive after a specific rupture — an affair, a betrayal of trust, a decision one made without the other. Many more arrive because of something slower: the same argument on a loop, a growing distance, a sense of living alongside each other rather than with each other.

Common threads include communication that turns into conflict, differences over parenting or money, intimacy that has faded, the strain of illness or bereavement, and the question of whether to stay together at all.

How the sessions work

We meet together, usually for 60 minutes. I am not there to referee or to decide who is right. My role is to slow down the pattern enough that both of you can see it happening — because most couples are not really arguing about the dishes.

Gestalt therapy is particularly suited to this, because it works with contact: how you reach each other, where that reaching gets blocked, and what each of you does when it does. Much of the useful material happens live, in the room, rather than in accounts of last Tuesday.

Both partners need to be willing to attend. Therapy is unlikely to help if one person has been brought under protest.

Counselling is not always about staying together

Couples counselling is sometimes assumed to have one correct outcome. It does not. Some couples reconnect. Some find a clearer, kinder way of separating, which matters enormously where children are involved.

What therapy offers is a considered decision rather than a decision made by attrition.
