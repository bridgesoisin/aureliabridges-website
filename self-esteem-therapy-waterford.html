Self-Esteem & Confidence Therapy in Waterford

Changing the relationship you have with yourself, not just what you think about yourself

Therapy for self-esteem works on the relationship you have with yourself — the internal voice, the standards you hold yourself to, and where they came from. I offer self-esteem and confidence therapy in Waterford, and online or by telephone, using a Gestalt and integrative approach.

Low self-esteem is rarely about confidence tricks

Most people with low self-esteem have already tried to think differently about themselves. They know the affirmations. They can list their achievements if pressed. And none of it touches the underlying conviction that they are, somehow, not quite enough.

That is because self-esteem is not really a set of beliefs. It is a relationship — a way of treating yourself that was usually learned early, from how you were treated, spoken to, or left alone.

What it looks like day to day

It might be apologising constantly. Finding it impossible to accept a compliment. Saying yes when you are already stretched, because the discomfort of disappointing someone outweighs your own exhaustion. Overworking to justify taking up space.

It can also look like perfectionism, or like avoiding anything you might not be immediately good at. Many people who appear highly capable are running on a fear of being found out.

How Gestalt therapy approaches it

We pay attention to how the critical voice actually operates — when it appears, what it says, whose voice it resembles. Bringing it into the open, rather than living inside it, tends to change its authority.

We also work with what happens in the room. How you speak about yourself, what you do when you are given attention, where you deflect — these are live examples of the pattern, available to explore as they happen.

The aim is not to replace harsh self-talk with forced positivity, but to develop a more accurate and more compassionate relationship with yourself, including the parts you find hard to accept.
