Grief & Bereavement Counselling in Waterford

A place to bring loss, at whatever stage and in whatever shape it comes

Grief counselling gives you somewhere to bring a loss without needing to manage anyone else's feelings about it. I offer bereavement and grief counselling in Waterford, and online or by telephone, working with loss of every kind — recent or many years old.

Grief does not follow a schedule

There is a widespread idea that grief moves through stages and finishes. In practice it moves in waves, doubles back, and arrives on days that make no sense. People often come to therapy worried that they are grieving wrongly — too much, too little, too long after.

Grief can also feel complicated when the relationship was difficult. Loss of someone you had unfinished business with can bring relief, guilt and anger alongside sadness, and those are harder to say aloud to family.

Loss that is not a death

Bereavement is the most recognised form of grief, but not the only one. The end of a marriage, a diagnosis that changes what your life will look like, infertility, a job that was part of who you were, leaving a country, an estrangement — all of these are losses, and all of them can be grieved.

These losses often get less acknowledgement from those around you, which can leave you grieving privately while appearing to carry on.

What the work involves

Much of grief work is simply having somewhere to put it, with someone who will not rush you toward feeling better. We give the loss room and attention, including the parts that feel unacceptable to say.

Gestalt therapy can also work with what remains unfinished — things unsaid, goodbyes that did not happen, the conversation you never got to have. Not to close it artificially, but to let something move.

Over time, most people find grief changes shape rather than disappearing. The aim is not to get over it, but to carry it in a way that leaves room for the rest of your life.
